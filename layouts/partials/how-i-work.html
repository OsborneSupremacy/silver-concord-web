How I Work

Straightforward advice, practical solutions, and no pressure.

Aligned with Your Goals

I recommend work that supports your goals—not projects designed to create billable hours. Nothing moves forward without your approval and buy-in, and all code I write for you belongs to you.

Simple, Appropriate Solutions

I favor proven tools that fit the use case. As an AWS specialist, I often use AWS services to reduce complexity, but I will recommend solutions outside the AWS ecosystem when they are the better choice. When an important need justifies added cost, I will explain why plainly.

Transparent Billing

Invoices are prompt, accurate, and detailed, with work descriptions, start and end times, and Jira ticket numbers when applicable. There are no surprise charges—and I do not nickel-and-dime clients for a brief email or routine meeting preparation.
